# Findings & Constraints

## Discovery Answers
1. **North Star:** An interactive, visually beautiful one-pager HTML app that generates a weekly home exercise plan.
2. **Integrations:** NotebookLM (via MCP) serves as the knowledge base for exercises.
3. **Source of Truth:** NotebookLM "Repwise" notebook.
4. **Delivery Payload:** A responsive, interactive HTML single-page application.
5. **Behavioral Rules:** Generate a weekly plan blending calisthenics and weights based on equipment, time, muscle groups, and goals.

## Research & Resources
